A few things you might be wondering.

Does my child need coding experience?

The proposed program welcomes first-time builders ages 6–10. Before booking, the school will confirm the right class for your child's age and experience.

What happens after I request a trial?

On the live site, WhatsApp will open with a ready-to-send message. The school will confirm availability and answer your questions. This demo shows the message without sending it.

Do we need to bring equipment?

The concept assumes classroom equipment is provided. The school will confirm what is included and anything your child should bring before the visit.
